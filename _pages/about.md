I am a PhD candidate in the [Division of Biostatistics](https://www.mcw.edu/departments/biostatistics) at the [Medical College of Wisconsin](https://www.mcw.edu/). I received my MS in Mathematics (Statistics emphasis) from [Boise State University](https://www.boisestate.edu/) and my BS in Statistics from the [University of Dhaka](https://www.du.ac.bd/).

I am interested in Bayesian variable selection for high-dimensional data with causal interpretation. As a graduate research assistant, I work with MCW investigators on clinical and genomic studies. Much of this work involves survival analysis, including competing risks, for transplant and oncology projects. I have also analyzed transcriptomic, proteomic, and microbiome data within high-dimensional settings.

Outside of research, I enjoy watching movies, trivia nights, traveling, reading, and spending time with my cats. If you would like to connect, please feel free to reach out via email at msalam[at]mcw[dot]edu or via the socials provided.
